## Context

As a product ecosystem grows, managing builds, versions and distribution channels becomes a problem of its own. Artifact Platform exists to be the **single source of truth** for every released software package.

## Goals

- Store immutable, clearly versioned artifacts.
- Verify integrity with checksums and signatures.
- Control upload / download permissions per product and customer.
- Integrate with CI/CD pipelines and the license management system.
