## Context

Commercial software needs a reliable way to license, activate and control usage — without getting in the way of legitimate users.

## Problems to solve

- Issue and manage licenses by product, plan and term.
- Activate / revoke licenses safely.
- Connect with Artifact Platform so packages are only distributed to legitimate users.
- Track the license lifecycle for operations and customer support.

## My role

Designing the overall architecture, setting the security direction and leading the development team. Details of specific protection mechanisms are not public.
